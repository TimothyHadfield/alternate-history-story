# Pure knowledge — ideas centuries before their time

Tim (2026-10-27): "pure knowledge-based things that might be impactful ... Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Earth Science, Astrology [taken as astronomy], light ... that are purely knowledge based and might not clearly have solid implications but are centuries before their time."

## How pure knowledge pays off (4 ways)
1. **Direct use**: it tells you what to do (mosquitoes cause malaria → drain the swamps).
2. **It saves dead ends**: no centuries wasted on alchemy, miasma theory, perpetual motion or bloodletting.
3. **Prophet power**: predictions that come true (an eclipse, a famine, a volcano) buy belief and political power.
4. **It speeds up discovery**: scientists who know the answer exists find it far faster.

## The catch: knowledge needs proof
History is full of ideas that were right and ignored: Aristarchus put the Sun at the center ~250 BC; Hero built a steam toy ~50 AD; Semmelweis proved handwashing and was mocked to death in an asylum. A claim without a demonstration is just a strange man talking.
**Story mechanic:** every knowledge drop goes **Claim → Demonstration → Adoption**, and the demonstration is the scene.

Payoff speed: ⚡ fast (years) · ⏳ medium (a generation) · 🌌 mostly awe/credibility.
Danger: ☠️ = heresy risk with the medieval Church.

## Biology
| Idea | What it changes | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| **Germs cause disease** (+ the microscope to prove it) | Sanitation, antiseptic surgery, clean water | ⚡ |
| ⭐ **Disease carriers**: mosquitoes → malaria/yellow fever; **rat fleas → plague**; body lice → typhus (the great army killer); dirty water → cholera/dysentery | Nets, drained swamps, rat control, delousing, clean wells. **Knowing fleas spread the Black Death (1347) could save a third of the population** | ⚡ |
| **Immune system / vaccination** | Smallpox first, then others | ⚡ |
| **Heredity (Mendel's genetics)** | Fast, planned breeding of crops, horses, cattle, sheep | ⏳ |
| **Nutrition & vitamins** | Scurvy, rickets, goiter, beriberi solved; healthier soldiers and sailors | ⚡ |
| **Blood circulation, blood types** | Safe transfusions (blood types can be tested by mixing drops on a plate) | ⏳ |
| **Ecology: populations, invasive species** | Sustainable fishing and forests; don't release rabbits or rats on islands; animals as pest control | ⏳ |
| **Evolution** ☠️ | Explains breeding and resistance; huge philosophical bomb | ⏳ / ☠️ |
| **Human anatomy, the brain, hormones** | Better surgery and medicine | ⏳ |

## Chemistry & physics of matter
| Idea | What it changes | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| **Atoms and elements** (no "four elements") | Ends alchemy; chemists stop chasing gold | ⏳ |
| **Conservation of mass** (Lavoisier) | Chemistry becomes a measuring science | ⏳ |
| **Oxygen and combustion** | Better furnaces, understanding of breathing and fire | ⚡ |
| **Periodic table** | Predicts undiscovered elements and their properties; tells you what to look for | ⏳ |
| **Electrons, charge** | Electricity makes sense instead of being magic | ⏳ |
| ⭐ **Toxic metals: lead and mercury poison you** | Stop lead pipes, lead-sweetened wine, mercury medicines and hat-making. Simple and saves many lives | ⚡ |
| **Radioactivity & decay** | Mostly *warning knowledge* (don't touch uranium/radium). Later: the age of the Earth, X-rays, nuclear power | 🌌 / ⏳ |
| **Energy conservation, thermodynamics** | No perpetual-motion projects; design efficient engines on purpose | ⏳ |
| **Air pressure & vacuum** | Pumps, steam engines, barometers, weather | ⚡ |

## Other fields worth adding
- **Economics**: don't debase the coinage (inflation), supply and demand, comparative advantage, compound interest, central banking.
- **Public health and government**: census, statistics, public works, merit-based officials (China's exams), rule of law.
- **Psychology & persuasion**: propaganda, morale, incentives. How to sell a strange idea to a king.
- **Engineering science**: strength of materials, the arch and truss, fluid flow (ship hulls), **lift** (gliders; Cayley flew one in 1849 with no engine).
- **Agriculture science**: soil chemistry, irrigation, pest control.

## Story ideas from this file

- **The plague arc**: he knows the Black Death is coming in 1347 and exactly how it spreads.
- **The heresy line**: some knowledge (Sun-centered sky, evolution, deep time) he keeps secret or hides inside religious language.
- **The codebreaker**: unbreakable codes give his kingdom a huge diplomatic and military edge.
