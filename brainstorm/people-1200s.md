## What stands out
- **Fame then ≠ fame now.** Then, the famous were **popes, caliphs, shahs and emperors**. Today we remember the **founders and thinkers**: Genghis, Francis, Fibonacci, Rumi.
- **Frederick II and Sultan al-Kamil were friends by letter.** They traded math and science puzzles and settled Jerusalem by treaty instead of war. Both are open-minded, science-loving rulers.
- **Many of the best scientific minds were in the Islamic world and China**, not Europe.
