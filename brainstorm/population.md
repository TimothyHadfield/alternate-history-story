# Population — maximum growth + maximum immigration

Tim (2026-10-27): "We basically want the maximum population growth as well as maximum immigration while maintaining positive growth."

Read as: grow as fast as possible from **both** births and newcomers, but never so fast that food, housing, order or living standards go backward.

## The two engines
**1. Natural growth (births minus deaths)**
- Medieval normal: ~0.1–0.5% a year (lots of births, lots of child deaths).
- With the hero's health package (clean water, midwives, vaccination, oral rehydration, food storage), child deaths drop from ~40% to ~10%. **Growth jumps to 2–3% a year** without anyone having more children.
- Push higher: land grants or tax breaks for families, free schooling (so kids are an asset), safe childbirth.
- Max realistic: **~3–3.5% a year** (doubles every ~20–23 years).

**2. Immigration**
- The USA's peak was ~1–1.5% of its population per year arriving (1900s). A small, well-organized society that *actively recruits* could aim for **2–3% a year**.
- **Combined: ~5–6% a year = doubling every ~12–14 years.** 50,000 people → ~1.6 million in 70 years.

## Who could come (1200s sources)

| **Persian & Central Asian craftsmen and scholars** | 1219–1258 | Fleeing Mongol massacres (Merv, Samarkand, Baghdad) |

| **Song Chinese** | 1270s | Fleeing the Mongol conquest by sea (many went to Vietnam and Southeast Asia) |
| **Jewish communities** | 1290 (England), 1306 (France) | Expelled; merchants, doctors, scholars |
| **Freed captives and slaves** | Ongoing | Buy people's freedom at slave markets, give them land and citizenship. Grateful, loyal citizens |
| **Famine victims** | 1258, 1315–17 | Anyone with stored grain can attract thousands |
| **Neighbors** | Always | People walk to wherever life is better: no tax gouging, no famine, a doctor |

*Which groups matter depends on the homeland. In southern Africa the nearby sources would be neighboring farming peoples, the Swahili coast, and freed captives from the Indian Ocean trade.*

## Absorbing them without breaking
Growth fails when newcomers outrun these limits. Track each one:
1. **Food per person** must keep rising (the granary rule: always 1–2 years of reserves).
2. **Housing and land:** surveyed plots ready *before* people arrive.
3. **Disease:** quarantine every arriving group (40 days) and vaccinate them. Immigrants historically brought epidemics.
4. **Language and belonging:** free language classes, mixed settlements (not ghettos), equal citizenship after a few years of service. Rome and the USA absorbed millions this way.
5. **Work:** every newcomer gets a job on day one (state projects are perfect: roads, mines, canals).
6. **Loyalty and secrets:** newcomers don't get into the weapons works or key industries until they've been citizens for a while. Some will be spies.

## The growth dashboard (what he'd watch every year)
Births · child deaths · arrivals · departures · food reserves (years) · food per person · housing waiting list · new citizens · disease outbreaks.

**The "positive growth" rule:** keep immigration at whatever level keeps food per person and reserves rising. If they drop, slow arrivals for a year.
