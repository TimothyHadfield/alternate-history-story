## 2026-10-27 (first session)
- Main character goes back to an ancient/old world with modern knowledge. How much impact could he make — diplomatic, technological, other?
- He gets into a position of power and tells people to do things that confuse them but change the world.
- **Favorite kind of change: simple things nobody thought of, with massive implications.**
- Things to bring: calculus, electricity basics, astrophysics, medicine, hygiene/health, powerful tech, steam engine, simple electric components, telescopes/glassware, chemical procedures.
- Pour resources into science, recreate modern inventions, find natural resources to grow wealth, lead his people into an industrial society in a medieval period.
- Framing (later): starts in a dystopian future; he's sent back to save the world from being destroyed and must develop technology as fast as possible "so they don't get destroyed".
